---
title: "Deductible (or excess)"
description: "The part of a covered claim that the policyholder is responsible for, as described in the policy."
related:
  - "/blog/does-home-insurance-cover-ev-charger/"
  - "/blog/ev-battery-total-loss-gap-coverage/"
---

A **deductible** is the amount a policyholder may have to pay toward a covered claim before the insurer pays its portion. **Excess** is a term often used for a similar concept in markets such as the United Kingdom.

The amount, when it applies and how it interacts with a particular loss depend on the policy. Some policies have different amounts for different coverages or events, so compare the written schedule rather than just the premium.
