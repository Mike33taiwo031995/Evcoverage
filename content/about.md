## About EV Insurance Guide

EV Insurance Guide is an independent educational resource for electric vehicle owners navigating auto insurance — coverage gaps, claims, and cost comparisons that standard advice often misses.

### What we cover

Real, specific EV insurance scenarios: coverage gaps in default policies, home charging equipment coverage, and how to compare quotes effectively — grounded in real cases and cited sources, not manufactured statistics.

### Our approach

We do not accept payment from insurance carriers for placement or reviews. Where we reference data, we cite the source and date. Where something is a hypothetical or illustrative example, we say so plainly.

### Disclaimer

Content on this site is for educational purposes only and does not constitute professional insurance, legal, or financial advice. Policy terms, coverage requirements, and regulations vary by state, carrier, and vehicle. Always confirm details with a licensed insurance professional before making coverage decisions.
