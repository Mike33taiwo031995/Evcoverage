### Information we collect

If you submit your name and email to receive our free guide, we use it only to deliver that guide and send occasional related emails. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with insurance carriers, brokers, or marketing networks.

### Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, and may display advertising through Google AdSense, which uses cookies to serve relevant ads. You can control ad personalization through [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

### Third-party services

We use Google Analytics (analytics), Meta Pixel (advertising measurement), and Google AdSense (advertising, once approved). Each operates under its own privacy policy.

### Your choices

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking via the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time via the link in any email we send.
