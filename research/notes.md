# Research Notes: EV Insurance Regional and Practical Guides

**Status:** researching
**Depth:** Standard

## Plan

- **Question:** What can EV owners reliably check about insurance by world region, quotes, policy questions, comparison and claim records?
- **Scope:** Six regional explainers plus four practical evergreen guides. No invented premium averages, blanket coverage promises or country-specific legal conclusions without direct evidence.
- **Audience:** EV owners comparing or preparing for insurance in their market.
- **Deliverable:** Ten Hugo pillar articles with inline source links, cited comparison tables/diagrams where useful, visible scope limits, and homepage links to the relevant guide.
- **Geography:** Use UN M49 regional groupings as the working convention. “America” means the United States when used as a country alias and routes to North America; Canada routes to North America, not Europe. Country examples remain explicitly country-specific.

## Coverage Checklist

- [ ] Explain what varies between countries and avoid treating any region as one insurance market.
- [ ] Distinguish compulsory motor liability from optional physical-damage/own-vehicle cover where sources support it.
- [ ] Explain battery, charging, repair, parts, loan and claim questions without promising coverage.
- [ ] Give readers a practical, privacy-conscious comparison and claim-document workflow.
- [ ] Cite every material local rule or numeric claim to current primary or high-quality sources.
- [ ] Avoid publishing regional premium rankings or averages without a comparable, dated dataset.
- [ ] Route regional names and country aliases to the correct region guide; Canada must not route to Europe.
- [ ] Make the homepage's dedicated guide links useful without removing the existing calculators, FAQ, or article hub.
