# Graphics standards for the queued articles

- Every article in the November 17–26 queue includes at least one table,
  two relevant pictures, one infographic and one chart.
- Homepage-linked guides retain three relevant pictures each.
- Use short public labels: “Illustrative image”, “Illustrative infographic”
  and “Illustrative chart”. Avoid long production-method notices in captions.
- Preserve important hypothetical-data, policy and safety qualifications.
- New numerical examples are explicitly hypothetical, never market rates,
  customer quotations, claims statistics or predicted outcomes.
- Charging, premium/deductible and GAP chart values are derived from the
  arithmetic already explained in those articles.
- Fire, storm, storage, body-estimate, exclusion-outcome and payment-fee examples
  use new, clearly labeled illustrative assumptions. They establish no coverage
  entitlement and describe no actual insurer's prices.
- Chart source data and builders were validated using the pinned Atta runtime.
  Locally hosted image exports need no third-party chart JavaScript in production.
- Process infographics summarize the article's own steps. They are not a
  substitute for emergency instructions or a technical repair procedure.
- Keep these same elements in later articles; never invent empirical data just
  to fill a chart. Label assumptions and explain what a chart cannot establish.
