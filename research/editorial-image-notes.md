# Editorial image provenance and use

Prepared November 17, 2026.

- Twenty-three photograph-style editorial illustrations were generated for this
  website's article updates. They are not documentary photographs, manufacturer
  diagrams, evidence of incidents or an insurer endorsement.
- Subjects: a sealed battery pack; professional battery assessment; storm parking;
  post-storm assessment; collision assessment; professional recovery; body-panel
  work; repair estimates; home charging; public charging; policy review; contract
  details; finance planning; vehicle valuation; six generic regional charging
  scenes; home charger equipment; battery warranty records; and quote comparison.
- The Tesla exterior illustration is derived from the site's existing
  `/images/blog-cars/tesla-model-y.jpg` asset, not a newly sourced press photograph.
  Its caption identifies it as an existing site illustration and not an official
  Tesla product image.
- Three relevant inline figures are included in each of fourteen guides linked
  directly from homepage sections and the U.S. state guide retained from the
  previous homepage layout. This includes all six regional guides.
- Two relevant inline figures remain in each of twelve other planned topics:
  ten scheduled articles, the expanded repair-shop guide and the flood guide.
- Images are reused where they explain the same subject; there are sixty-nine
  inline placements across twenty-four distinct image subjects.
- Regional scenes are generic illustrations, not photographs identifying an
  actual location. Each regional caption says this explicitly. A depicted setting
  does not represent every country in that guide.
- Two legacy hotlinked pictures (home charger and battery total loss) were
  replaced with local responsive illustrations. All inline pictures in these
  twenty-seven articles are local files, not third-party image hotlinks.
- Every placement includes descriptive alternative text, an explanatory caption
  and an illustration credit.
- Images preserve their original composition and aspect ratio. Responsive WebP
  sizes are 480, 768 and 1024 pixels wide; none is upscaled.
- Width and height reserve layout space; lazy loading and asynchronous decoding
  avoid forcing all article images to load at once.
- Image descriptions and captions do not diagnose damage, establish coverage or
  replace manufacturer repair and towing instructions.
- No new third-party editorial photograph was copied or hotlinked.
- Article claims, tables, source attributions and original publication dates are
  preserved. Fifteen newly illustrated guides have a November 17 modification
  date and a topic-relevant card image; all other front matter is preserved.
- This image pass is not a fresh factual audit of inherited legacy prose.
