The EV-specific questions

Know what to ask before you need to ask.

Use these as a starting point. Answers depend on policy wording, local rules and details of your vehicle.

01 / BATTERY

What happens if the battery is damaged?

Ask how the policy treats battery damage after a collision, fire, water event or other covered loss. Clarify whether the battery is owned, leased or covered by a separate warranty, and how repair versus replacement decisions are made.

Read the EV coverage library →
02 / REPAIR TIME

Where can it be repaired?

Confirm whether an approved repair network can work on your make and model, what happens if specialist parts or technicians are not locally available, and how transport or temporary mobility is handled.

03 / AT HOME

Is the charger home equipment?

A fixed charger and a portable cable may be treated differently. Ask how installation, declared equipment, limits, deductibles and cause of damage affect cover across home and auto policies.

Read the home charger guide →
04 / FINANCING

Could a loan outlast the car's value?

Compare your current lender balance with a recent value estimate. If the balance is higher, ask what options apply in your market; the calculator only shows arithmetic and does not determine coverage eligibility.

Renewal and claim preparation

Keep the useful details together.

Gather your policy schedule, vehicle identification and modifications, battery or charger paperwork, lender balance, dated vehicle value source, repair preferences, and notes from previous claims. Save claim photos and correspondence securely.

A few useful clarifications

Good questions deserve careful answers.

There is no single worldwide EV policy. Ask your insurer to point to the policy language for your location.

Does an EV need a different kind of insurance?

There is no universal answer. Battery, repair arrangements, replacement parts and charging equipment can raise questions worth checking in policy documents.

Does home insurance cover a home charger?

It depends on location, installation type, policy wording, limits and cause of damage. A fixed unit may be treated differently from portable equipment. Tell your home insurer about the installation and ask for the answer in writing. Read the charger explainer.

Can you tell me EV insurance costs in my area?

Not reliably at present. This guide does not publish verified comparable local prices. Request current quotes with matching details from insurers serving your market.

Does the gap calculator say whether I need additional cover?

No. It subtracts the value and loan balance you enter. It does not account for settlement rules, eligibility, exclusions, local availability or product terms.

Is the U.S. material a worldwide benchmark?

No. The companion handbook and workbook refer to U.S.-only loan-to-value and comparison tools and U.S. state/model information. They are not global figures or representative costs for other markets.

Is this insurance, legal or financial advice?

No. This is educational material, not professional insurance, legal or financial advice. Confirm current local requirements and your policy with a licensed professional.
